What this proves

Signature verification confirms signer authorization. It does not prove the truthfulness of the underlying real-world data. Blockchain does not prove the worker data is true — only that the claim came from an authorized signer.
In this prototype you click the approval button to demonstrate the check. In a real version, an authorized platform would create and sign the attestation automatically after the worker gives consent — no manual approval step.

Trust rehabilitation

A missed payment does not permanently block a worker. Resumed repayment moves them back up the ladder.

  • Lower balance: After your first payment, the amount you owe goes down.
  • Same cycle next month: A new month starts the same repayment cycle again.
  • Step by step: Each completed cycle unlocks more credit.

How the score is calculated

1. Worker data → 2. Verification → 3. Signal normalization → 4. Weighted scoring → 5. Score band → 6. Loan policy → 7. Repayment performance → 8. Repayment ladder → 9. Recovery.

Key prototype rules

All exact score movements, loan amounts and recovery percentages are prototype policy choices for this hackathon and are not empirically validated lending rules. Data privacy: workers control what data is shared; off-platform signals are consent-based; no religion, caste, gender, race, political affiliation or social data is used anywhere in scoring.

Audit trail
